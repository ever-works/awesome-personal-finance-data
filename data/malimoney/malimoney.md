## Pricing

Freemium. A free sample with demo accounts (no signup), 30+ calculators and a daily spending roast are free. Voice Pro is $4.99/month and Premium is $29.99/month.

## Features

- Links bank accounts read-only through Plaid (US).
- Ask by voice or text, and Mali answers out loud in real time.
- 30+ calculators, including retirement odds, Social Security timing, Roth conversions, debt payoff and FIRE.
- A public MCP server (`https://malimoney.com/v1/mcp`, no auth) exposes the calculators to AI assistants.

Educational, not financial advice. Closed source.
